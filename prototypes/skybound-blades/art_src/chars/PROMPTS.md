# 浮空劍域：角色原畫提示詞（第一批）

本作原創角色，不沿用 hero-horde-idle 的人物或圖。風格依 implementation-plan §10：3～3.5 頭身 HD 插畫，輪廓清楚、柔和分色（cel shading），**不是像素風**。斜俯視、朝右下，單方向＋鏡像。

共同 spec：`--finish hd`、`--facing right`、`--key magenta`、`--genre "a Ragnarok-Online-style HD 2.5D action RPG set in floating sky-tower ruins"`、
`--view "elevated three-quarter overhead view about 35 degrees, body turned diagonally toward the lower-right"`、
`--ban "pixel art" --ban "magenta, pink or red-violet colours on the character" --ban "cast shadow on the ground" --ban "text or logos"`。

## 1. captain（隊長・單手劍，玩家角色）— `--class hero --pronoun he`
- subject: a young sky-tower adventurer swordsman, 3-head-tall chibi-leaning proportions
- identity: short windswept dark-teal hair, bright amber eyes, determined smile; light steel shoulder guard on the left shoulder only; sky-blue short tunic over cream undershirt; brown leather belt with a small pouch; dark grey trousers; brown boots; a long cream travelling scarf trailing behind; a straight one-handed arming sword with a plain brass guard held in the right hand; no shield
- pose: ready stance, sword held low and forward, weight on the front foot
- recap: young teal-haired swordsman in a sky-blue tunic, cream scarf and single steel pauldron, holding a one-handed arming sword

## 2. guardian（盾衛・槌盾）— `--class hero --pronoun she`
- subject: a stocky tower guardian knight, 3-head-tall proportions
- identity: tied-back copper-brown hair, calm steady eyes; rounded steel half-plate over a deep teal surcoat with a pale gold wing emblem; chainmail sleeves; a large round wooden shield rimmed in iron held on the left arm, painted teal with a gold wing; a short flanged iron mace in the right hand; sturdy grey boots
- pose: guard stance, shield raised to chest height, mace ready at the hip
- recap: stocky copper-haired guardian in steel half-plate and teal surcoat, carrying a large round teal shield and a flanged mace

## 3. medic（援護・野戰醫護）— `--class hero --pronoun he`
- subject: a nimble field medic scout, 3-head-tall proportions
- identity: messy ash-blond hair under a moss-green hood, gentle grey eyes, small round spectacles; moss-green short cape over an off-white field coat with many pockets; a cross-body satchel full of rolled linen bandages and herb bundles; a short straight sword sheathed on the hip; tan gloves; soft brown boots; no religious symbols, no red cross
- pose: light-footed stance, one hand pulling a bandage roll from the satchel
- recap: ash-blond field medic in a moss-green hooded cape and off-white coat, with spectacles and a satchel of bandage rolls

## 4. meadow_slime（草原史萊姆）— `--class mob --pronoun it`
- subject: a round meadow slime monster, about knee height to the hero
- identity: translucent jade-green jelly body with a soft highlight, a single clover sprout and two tiny leaves growing from the top, dark bead eyes and a small open mouth, a few grass blades and a pebble suspended inside the jelly
- pose: squashed ready-to-hop shape
- recap: translucent jade-green slime with a clover sprout on top and grass blades suspended inside

## 5. briar_boar（荊棘野豬）— `--class mob --pronoun it`
- subject: a stout wild boar monster, about waist height to the hero
- identity: shaggy dark-brown fur, a crest of tangled thorny briar vines with small white flowers growing along its spine, two curved ivory tusks, small fierce amber eyes, mossy hooves
- pose: head lowered, about to charge
- recap: dark-brown boar with a thorny briar crest along its spine and curved ivory tusks

## 6. ruin_hound（遺跡獵犬）— `--class mob --pronoun it`
- subject: a lean ancient ruin hound, about waist height to the hero
- identity: slate-grey short fur, a cracked pale-stone mask covering the upper face with two glowing cyan eye slits, a stone collar carved with faint cyan runes, long legs, a whip-thin tail with a stone tip
- pose: low prowling stance, front paw lifted
- recap: lean slate-grey hound with a cracked stone mask, glowing cyan eye slits and a runed stone collar
